## 2. Answer 3: fetch timeout

- `App.fetchTimeout()` returns `max(globalFetchTimeout, 2*a.config.Interval)`, and `doFetch` uses it. It is read on the UI goroutine, before the command runs.
- The other commands (restart, config, certificates) keep the fixed 10 s: they do not wait on a poll.
- Tests:
  - `TestFetchTimeout_OutlastsASlowInterval`: 1 s gives 10 s, and **30 s gives 60 s**;
  - `TestDoFetch_UsesTheFetchTimeout` checks that a fetcher called with a 30 s interval receives a deadline more than 50 s away.
- The remote client still honours its caller's deadline: this answer keeps the `context` contract intact.
